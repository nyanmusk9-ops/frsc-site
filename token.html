6 · What we launched

Purpose of the token we launched

Same name. Same seal. Same story. New chain — so the legacy has a place to live.

We launched $FRSC on BNB Chain as a community token whose job is cultural continuity: keep the Federal Reserve System Coin narrative, imagery, and critique in active circulation. It is not the 2014 scrypt chain restarted. It is a new issuance built to carry the legacy forward where people actually are now.

Purpose What that means
Preserve the lore Give the Bitcointalk ANN, the OG website story, and the btcpay86 → CZ record a living home — not only archive links.
Keep the critique public The Fed / QE / "money printer" theme was the point in 2014. The token exists to keep that conversation visible, shareable, and hard to erase.
Honor the motto Community posture: no hidden mint games in the spirit of the original punchline — WILL NOT BE DEVALUED COINS. Fair launch norms, 0/0 tax, liquidity burned / contract renounced where stated.
Make participation simple Unlike the old mineable chain, today's $FRSC is a BEP-20 people can hold and trade on BNB Chain — the infrastructure of this era, not 2014 pools.
Stay honest about affiliation This is a community continuation of the concept. It is not endorsed by CZ, Binance, the Federal Reserve, or the original 2014 operators as an official product.
