The lore

The oldest CZ coin nobody remembered

Federal Reserve Coin wasn't a fork of a fork. It was a joke about central banking, shipped by the man who would go on to build the largest exchange on earth.

  1. Dec 2013

    The Fed blinks

    On December 18, 2013 the Fed announced the taper of quantitative easing — monthly bond purchases cut from $10B to $75B. Five years of the "printing machine" had a name, and crypto had a punchline.

  2. Jan 2014

    $FRSC is born

    FRSC launches on Bitcointalk, timed to the Federal Reserve's 100th birthday — a virtual currency dedicated to a century of "printing", and to the people who kept paying for it. Tagline on the original site: WILL NOT BE DEVALUED COINS.

  3. 2014

    btcpay86 is CZ

    The launching account, btcpay86, is CZ himself. His post history reads like a résumé of early crypto: hands-on wallet support, exchange plumbing, and — translate the page and search for it — blockchain.info, where CZ worked before Binance.

  4. 2025

    The seal returns

    The original chain faded, the domain lapsed, the site survives only in the Wayback Machine. The ticker doesn't die. $FRSC is reborn on BNB Chain — CZ's coin, on CZ's chain, holding the same seal.

Current FAQs

Informing the public about the Federal Reserve Coin.

Is $FRSC officially connected to CZ or Binance?

No. $FRSC is a community memecoin built on the 2014 history of Federal Reserve Coin. It is not endorsed by, affiliated with or operated by CZ, Binance, BNB Chain or the Federal Reserve System.

Is this the same token as the 2014 coin?

No. The 2014 FRSC was a standalone scrypt proof-of-work chain. This is a BEP-20 token on BNB Chain that revives the ticker, the seal and the story. The original chain is no longer maintained.

How do I know btcpay86 is CZ?

Read the account's public post history yourself — it's linked in The receipts. Translate the page, then search for blockchain.info: the account answers support questions for blockchain.info and discusses working there, which matches CZ's role before Binance. Draw your own conclusion.

What's the tax?

0% on buys, 0% on sells. Permanently. There is no mint function, no blacklist and no pause switch.

Where is the liquidity?

Burned at launch. The LP tokens are sent to the dead address, so the pool cannot be pulled.

Will $FRSC be devalued?

Per the 2014 homepage, in capitals, across the seal: WILL NOT BE DEVALUED COINS.
