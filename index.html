Bitcointalk, January 2014 · Scrypt PoW chain (defunct) · BEP-20 revival on BNB Chain

A joke coin timed to the Fed's 100th birthday. The punchline outlived the chain.

In January 2014, on the Federal Reserve's centennial, a Bitcointalk account named btcpay86 launched Federal Reserve Coin — a virtual currency dedicated to a century of "printing," and to the people who kept paying for it. The original chain faded. The domain lapsed. The site survives in the Wayback Machine. The ticker returned on BNB Chain.

The launching account was btcpay86 (user 202137). The public record is below. Draw your own conclusion.

Historical record

The coin that mocked the money printer

Federal Reserve Coin wasn't a fork of a fork. It was a joke about central banking, posted in public, then nearly forgotten — until the ticker came back.

  1. Dec 2013

    The Fed blinks

    On December 18, 2013 the Fed announced the taper of quantitative easing — monthly bond purchases cut from $85B toward $75B. Five years of the "printing machine" had a name, and crypto had a punchline.

  2. Jan 2014

    $FRSC is born

    FRSC launches on Bitcointalk, timed to the Federal Reserve's 100th birthday — a virtual currency dedicated to a century of "printing", and to the people who kept paying for it. Tagline on the original site: WILL NOT BE DEVALUED COINS.

  3. 2014

    The launching account: btcpay86

    User 202137 posted under the name btcpay86. The post history reads like early-crypto infrastructure work: wallet support, exchange plumbing, and — if you translate the page and search — repeated references to blockchain.info, where Changpeng Zhao (CZ) worked before Binance. The record is public. Attribution is for the reader.

  4. 2015–2024

    The missing years

    The scrypt chain went quiet. Mining interest dried up. federalreservecoin.org lapsed; about 31 Wayback captures remain between January 2014 and November 2020 — wallet downloads, pool links, and the disclaimer: "This's Not The Fed's Official Website." The artifact outlived the network.

  5. 2025–

    The seal returns

    The ticker doesn't die. $FRSC is reborn as a BEP-20 on BNB Chain — same seal, same joke, new issuance rules. Community research, not an official revival by any named founder or exchange.

Current FAQs

Informing the public about the Federal Reserve Coin.

Is $FRSC officially connected to CZ or Binance?

No. $FRSC is a community memecoin built on the 2014 history of Federal Reserve Coin. It is not endorsed by, affiliated with or operated by CZ, Binance, BNB Chain or the Federal Reserve System.

Is this the same token as the 2014 coin?

No. The 2014 FRSC was a standalone scrypt proof-of-work chain. This is a BEP-20 token on BNB Chain that revives the ticker, the seal and the story. The original chain is no longer maintained.

Was btcpay86 really CZ?

This portal does not assert official identity. It points to the public post history linked in The receipts. Translate the page, then search for blockchain.info: the account answers support questions and discusses working there — which matches CZ's publicly known role before Binance. Read the record and decide for yourself.

What's the tax?

0% on buys, 0% on sells. Permanently. There is no mint function, no blacklist and no pause switch.

Where is the liquidity?

Burned at launch. The LP tokens are sent to the dead address, so the pool cannot be pulled.

Will $FRSC be devalued?

Per the 2014 homepage, in capitals, across the seal: WILL NOT BE DEVALUED COINS.
